ALPHAONE AI -- STRATEGY RESEARCH V3: ROBUST BTC/USDT FUTURES STRATEGY DISCOVERY
======================================================================
Generated by scripts/research_v3_discovery.py (Stage 1: cheap full-period
screen) and scripts/research_v3_validation.py + scripts/research_v3_sensitivity_extra.py
(Stage 2: full train/val/OOS rigor, walk-forward, regime, sensitivity,
bootstrap, cost-stress) against real historical BTC/USDT data
(alphaone_research.db, Binance-sourced, ~3 years). Every number in this
report is real, unedited script output. No synthetic/fabricated inputs or

This pass's objective was NOT to force ten strategies. It was to discover
genuinely robust BTC/USDT futures strategies and report however many the
evidence actually supports -- see Section J for the final count.

======================================================================
METHODOLOGY (two-stage, building on V2)
======================================================================

STAGE 1 -- cheap, single-pass, full-period backtest (real fees/slippage/
funding, no train/val/OOS split) across a wide pool of genuinely distinct
mechanisms. Purpose: candidate reduction only, never a profitability
verdict. A candidate needs full-period PF >= 1.0 to advance to Stage 2.

STAGE 2 -- applied ONLY to Stage 1 survivors, reusing V2's exact
chronological 60/20/20 TRAIN/VALIDATION/OOS methodology
(scripts/research_v2_rigorous.py): any free parameter is grid-searched,
selected on VALIDATION only (>=15-trade eligibility floor), then FROZEN
and evaluated exactly once on OOS -- never re-tuned after seeing OOS.
Also computed on the frozen OOS run: 4-fold walk-forward, long/short
breakdown, bull/bear + high/low-vol regime attribution, parameter-neighbor
sensitivity, a 500x trade-order bootstrap, cross-strategy correlation, and
one dimension NEW in V3 that V2 did not have: a COST-ROBUSTNESS STRESS
TEST (2x taker fee, 3x slippage, 2x average funding) re-run on the frozen
OOS strategy, to check the edge isn't a fragile artifact of the baseline
cost assumptions.

Cost model (baseline): taker fee 0.04%, slippage 1bps, funding avg 0.01%
(services/backtester/engine.py's ExchangeSpec default -- same model every
backtest in this codebase uses).

Existing protections honored throughout: S05 (Donchian+ADX, 4h) was NOT
re-implemented or retuned -- its production code is untouched. S06
(Supertrend+ATR, 4h) was independently re-evaluated on the CURRENT dataset
(not assumed to remain eligible just because V2 promoted it) -- see
Section D.

--- V3_KAMA_TREND_4H (NEW, PRODUCTION_ELIGIBLE) ---
Timeframe: 4h | Data mode: CLOSED_CANDLE | Frozen param: er_period=10 (fast=2, slow=30 fixed)
Mechanism: Kaufman's Adaptive Moving Average -- its smoothing constant
adapts to the market's own efficiency ratio (net move / sum of bar-to-bar
moves over er_period bars), speeding up in a clean trend and flattening in
chop. Entry on a price/KAMA cross where the KAMA line itself has already
sloped in the cross direction over the last 3 bars (avoids entering on a
cross that's really just noise around a flat KAMA).
Entry: close, on price crossing KAMA with KAMA already sloping that way.
SL: entry -/+ 2.0x ATR(14). TP1/TP2/TP3: entry +/- 1.5/2.5/3.5x ATR(14).
Signal logic: LONG/SHORT/NO_TRADE, deterministic, no lookahead (KAMA's
recursive step at row i only reads row i-1's own value and row i's inputs).

--- V3_RANGE_EXPANSION_4H (NEW, PRODUCTION_ELIGIBLE, regime-dependent) ---
Timeframe: 4h | Data mode: CLOSED_CANDLE | Frozen param: tr_ratio_mult=2.0 (range_period=20 fixed)
Mechanism: a true-range bar at least 2.0x the 20-bar average true range,
that ALSO breaks the prior 20-bar high/low, marks a genuine breakout with
real participation -- distinct from S05 (no volatility-expansion
precondition) and V3_HMA/KAMA (no breakout-of-a-level requirement at all).
Entry: close, on a range-expansion bar breaking the prior 20-bar high/low.
SL: entry -/+ 2.0x ATR(14). TP1/TP2/TP3: entry +/- 1.5/2.5/3.5x ATR(14).
REGIME-DEPENDENT (disclosed, not hidden): profitable specifically in bear/
high-volatility conditions, loses money in bull/low-volatility conditions
-- see Section G.
